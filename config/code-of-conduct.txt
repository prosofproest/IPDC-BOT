INVICTUS PROJECT DEVELOPMENT CENTRE (IPDC)
OFFICIAL CODE OF CONDUCT

1. DECLARATION & COMMITMENT

By becoming a member of the INVICTUS PROJECT DEVELOPMENT CENTRE (IPDC), every individual agrees to maintain discipline, professionalism, integrity, teamwork, and mutual respect.

All members are expected to contribute towards the organization's objectives and uphold its standards.

2. CODE OF CONDUCT

All members must:
- Treat every member with respect and maintain professional communication.
- Refrain from harassment, bullying, discrimination, threats, or abusive behavior.
- Respect the decisions and instructions of authorized Project Managers and Administrators.
- Maintain confidentiality of internal discussions, project resources, and sensitive information.
- Avoid plagiarism and acknowledge the contributions of others.
- Use IPDC channels and resources responsibly.
- Work collaboratively and fulfill assigned responsibilities.
- Avoid any activity that damages the reputation or functioning of IPDC.

3. ATTENDANCE & PARTICIPATION

- Every member must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in assigned meetings, project sessions, and organizational activities.
- Members are expected to actively participate in their assigned teams and projects.
- Members must inform their respective Project Manager in advance regarding unavoidable absences.
- Repeated absence, inactivity, or failure to fulfill responsibilities may lead to disciplinary action.

4. DISCIPLINARY ACTION

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in:
- Verbal Warning
- Written Warning
- Temporary Suspension
- Removal from Project Responsibilities
- Termination of Membership

The disciplinary action will depend on the severity and frequency of the violation.

5. TERMINATION

Membership may be terminated in cases of:
- Serious or repeated violations of the Code of Conduct.
- Harassment, misconduct, or inappropriate behavior.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Persistent failure to fulfill assigned responsibilities.
- Actions that negatively affect the organization or its members.

The final decision regarding termination shall be made by the authorized IPDC Administration.

6. DECLARATION OF ACCEPTANCE

By accepting this Code of Conduct, I confirm that I have read, understood, and agreed to follow the rules, policies, and standards of the INVICTUS PROJECT DEVELOPMENT CENTRE.

I understand that failure to comply may result in disciplinary action, including termination of membership.
